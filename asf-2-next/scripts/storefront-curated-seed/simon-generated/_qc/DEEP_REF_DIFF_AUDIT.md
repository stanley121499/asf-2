## Why this exists

A buyer must not receive a **different-looking product** than the PDP. Catalog lies that drive returns and trust damage include:

- **Strap width / thickness / gauge** (ribbon vs physical wide webbing / elastic)
- **Hardware identity** (fabric-covered buckle vs bare metal)
- **Heel construction** (invented padded collar vs slim strip/loop; missing vertical webbing)
- **Toe last** (round/almond vs soft-square)
- **Colorway / sole color** (wrong CW fallback; brown sole where cream is truth)

Prior QC notes often PASS’d for “tidy / no pillow / covered buckle family” while under-weighting **mass and construction fidelity**. This pass grades harshly against physical WhatsApp refs (+ colorway canon where no physical exists). Prefer over-listing diffs.
