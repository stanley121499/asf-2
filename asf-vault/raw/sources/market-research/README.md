## Important limitations

- **Prices and fees change.** Always confirm on the vendor’s official pricing, Shopee/Lazada Seller Centre, and your payment gateway’s schedule.
- **FX:** Many platforms bill in **USD**; MYR equivalents below are **illustrative** (roughly USD 1 ≈ MYR 4.2–4.5 depending on date and bank).
- **Tax:** SST and cross-border billing may apply to subscriptions and services; this pack does not replace accounting advice.

## Source types

Material is drawn from **official vendor pages** where possible, plus **secondary summaries** (blogs, partners) flagged where used. Secondary sources can lag or simplify; prefer Seller Centre and checkout flows for fee rules.
