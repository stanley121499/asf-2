# Marketplaces: Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop (Malaysia seller lens)

**Updated:** April 2026  

Many **first-time** Malaysian sellers never build a website at all — they start here. Owned-store platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce, EasyStore, etc.) compete for merchants who want **margin**, **customer data**, and **brand control** after marketplace traction.

## Why include marketplaces in “competitor” research

- They set **consumer expectations** for shipping speed, vouchers, and checkout trust.
- **Fee schedules** influence whether sellers can afford a parallel DTC channel.
- **Omnichannel tools** (EasyStore, SiteGiant, Shopify apps) exist largely to **sync** with these venues.

---

## Shopee Malaysia

**Seller hub:** Shopee Seller Centre / Seller Education Hub (always use **official** announcements for fee tables).

### What sellers get

- Massive **traffic**, campaigns, vouchers, live streaming, and built-in logistics options.
- **Seller tools** for ads, chat, promotions, and analytics.

### Marketing integrations (how sellers drive traffic inside the ecosystem)


| Lever                  | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **In-app ads**         | **Shopee Ads** / search ranking products—deducted from seller balance or card; separate from commission math.                                                                                           |
| **Live + short video** | Shopee Live, TikTok-style content on product pages; ties to vouchers and flash slots.                                                                                                                   |
| **External pixels**    | Less relevant than on owned stores; **attribution** is mostly platform-internal. Sellers still run **Meta / Google ads to Shopee listings** in some categories—track ROAS carefully vs on-platform ads. |
| **Chat**               | Built-in buyer chat; auto-replies and templates; critical for conversion on MY marketplaces.                                                                                                            |


### Fees (conceptual — verify in Seller Centre)

Fee structures **change** and are **category-dependent**. Summaries in 2025–2026 describe:

- A **transaction fee** on completed orders (blog summaries cite figures around **~3.5% + SST** → **~3.78% effective** on chargeable amounts — **confirm live**).
- **Marketplace commission** that may apply after **thresholds** (for example **100 completed orders** or **120 days** from first listing in some policies) for non-Mall sellers.
- **Category-based commission** bands; optional programmes (cashback / free shipping packages) can add **extra percentage points**.

**Rule:** Before pricing products, model **transaction fee + commission + SST on fees + shipping subsidies + ads**.

### Strengths

- Fastest path to **orders** for many categories.
- Strong **mobile** shopping behavior in Malaysia.

### Weaknesses

- **Margin compression** from fees and promotions arms race.
- **Platform rules** and competition on identical SKUs.

## Lazada Malaysia

**Seller hub:** Lazada Seller Center.

### What sellers get

Similar marketplace value prop: **traffic**, campaigns, Lazada Logistics ecosystem, brand / mall programs for official sellers.

### Fees

Lazada’s **commission and payment handling fees** vary by **seller type** (marketplace vs LazMall), **category**, and promotions. **Do not rely on this file** for fee tables — pull the **current** PDF / Seller Center page.

### Strengths / watch-outs

Same pattern as Shopee: reach vs margin, with **category** and **mall** programs affecting trust and fees.

---

## TikTok Shop Malaysia

**Seller hub:** TikTok Shop seller academy / seller center.

### What sellers get

- **Content-driven** discovery (short video, live) tightly coupled to checkout.
- Growing share of younger shoppers’ product discovery time.

### Fees

TikTok Shop fee schedules are **region- and category-specific** and change with campaigns. Use **official seller documentation** for Malaysia.

### Strengths

- Merchants with **organic content skills** can scale faster than SEO-only DTC.
- Impulse purchases align with certain product types.

### Watch-outs

- Content production burden; **policy** risk on categories and claims.
- Need to reconcile **inventory** with other channels if selling omnichannel.

## Practical comparison vs owned store


| Dimension  | Marketplaces                       | Owned store (Shopify / Woo / local SaaS)                     |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Traffic    | Platform brings demand             | You buy ads / SEO / social                                   |
| Fees       | Commissions + transaction + promos | Hosting/SaaS + gateway + apps                                |
| Data       | Limited vs DTC                     | Better access to customer relationship (if implemented well) |
| Operations | Platform rules                     | You own policies and CX                                      |
