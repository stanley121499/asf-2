# Malaysia- and SEA-focused e-commerce SaaS (EasyStore, SiteGiant)

**Updated:** April 2026  

These platforms are **common choices** when merchants want **MYR pricing**, **local integrations**, and **omnichannel** (webstore + Shopee/Lazada/TikTok + POS) without assembling WordPress or paying global USD SaaS prices.

---

## EasyStore

**Site:** [https://www.easystore.co/en-my/](https://www.easystore.co/en-my/)  
**Pricing page:** [https://www.easystore.co/en-my/pricing](https://www.easystore.co/en-my/pricing)

### Positioning

Positions as **unified commerce**: online store, **marketplace sync**, social channels, **POS**, **brand app**, marketing automation, loyalty, and messaging (WhatsApp / Messenger / etc.). Official copy states **no transaction fees** on plans (verify definitions: subscription vs gateway fees).

### Pricing (from official Malaysia pricing page, April 2026 fetch)

Displayed as **pay monthly** vs **pay yearly** (yearly is lower per month):


| Plan         | Monthly display                                               | Yearly-equivalent monthly (shown on site) |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Standard** | RM 249 / mo                                                   | RM 199 / mo                               |
| **Business** | RM 499 / mo                                                   | RM 399 / mo                               |
| **Growth**   | RM 899 crossed → **RM 497 / mo** (promo-style layout on page) | RM 699 crossed → **RM 497 / mo**          |
| **Success**  | RM 1,199 / mo                                                 | RM 999 / mo                               |


**Caveat:** The Growth row on the page had overlapping strike-through numbers in the fetched HTML; **confirm in checkout** before quoting externally.

### Feature highlights (marketing / compare table on site)

- **Marketplaces:** Shopee, TikTok Shop, Lazada, PGMall, Zalora (per site).
- **Social / live:** Facebook, Instagram, YouTube Shopping (some marked “coming soon”), Live selling.
- **Chat:** WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram, WeChat, LINE.
- **Retail:** POS, shopping app, multi-location inventory on higher tiers, staff roles.
- **Marketing:** Broadcasts, engagement automation, affiliate / referral, bundles.
- **Payments / logistics:** “Major payments and logistics providers” — verify exact gateways for your tier.

#### Plan ladder nuance (from comparison table)

- **Standard:** Several retail and “unified” marketing rows are marked **online only** (e.g. some loyalty and engagement features).
- **Business / Growth / Success:** More rows flip to **unified** (same promotions and customer view across web, POS, app).

### Strengths

- **All-in-one** narrative for Malaysian omnichannel retailers.
- **Local channel coverage** (Shopee / TikTok / Lazada) is a differentiator vs generic US builders.
- **No platform transaction fee** claim simplifies margin math vs Shopify’s third-party surcharge model (you still pay PSP fees).

### Weaknesses / watch-outs

- **Price ladder** rises quickly for advanced retail and automation tiers.
- **Platform lock-in** (export, custom workflows) should be evaluated like any SaaS.
- **Theme / builder flexibility** may be narrower than WooCommerce or Shopify Liquid + apps.

### Billing (FAQ on site)

States payment via **PayPal**, **Visa/Master**, and **online banking (FPX)**; **7-day refund** for new subscriptions (per FAQ on pricing page).

## SiteGiant

**Site:** [https://sitegiant.my/](https://sitegiant.my/)

### Positioning

Long-running Malaysian **omnichannel** vendor (public materials cite **10,000+ merchants**, operations since **2013**). Beyond a webstore, they sell **ERP**, **WMS**, **retail POS**, and **OMO** (online-merge-offline) solutions — aimed at sellers who are serious about **marketplaces** and operations.

### Product surface (from public marketing)

- **Webstore builder** with large theme library (marketing cites **70+** themes).
- **Multichannel:** Listings and sync to many marketplaces (Shopee, Lazada, etc.); training / onboarding emphasis.
- **Integrations:** Marketing claims **50+** integrations and sync back for inventory / orders.
- **Enterprise-style modules:** ERP, warehouse, retail — for scaling sellers.

### Functions and integrations (granular — typical SiteGiant stack)

SiteGiant is **modular** (webstore vs ERP vs WMS vs POS). Exact menus depend on products purchased; below reflects **public positioning** and partner summaries.

#### Sales channels and social


| Area                | Typical capabilities (marketing)                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Marketplaces**    | Sync/list to **Shopee**, **Lazada**, **Lelong**, **11street**, **YouBeli**, plus regional/international names in older partner pages (**Amazon**, **eBay**, **Qoo10**, etc.) — **verify current** supported list with sales. |
| **Social commerce** | **Facebook** shop/live style workflows, **Instagram** selling support in “Social Commerce” messaging.                                                                                                                        |
| **Webstore**        | Branded site, themes, checkout, payments, couriers (MY-focused).                                                                                                                                                             |
| **External carts**  | Some materials mention connecting **Shopify** or **WooCommerce** into the wider SiteGiant / Commerce Asia ecosystem — treat as **integration project**, not default.                                                         |


#### Operations and back office


| Module              | Role                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **SiteGiant ERP**   | Central **OMS**-style layer: multichannel orders, product master, often accounting sync hooks — aimed at sellers drowning in spreadsheets.         |
| **WMS**             | Warehouse operations, picking/packing, stock accuracy for higher volume.                                                                           |
| **Retail / POS**    | Brick-and-mortar tie-in with online stock (OMO narrative).                                                                                         |
| **Accounting sync** | Listed in footer/navigation as a product line — confirm which accounting packages (AutoCount, SQL, Xero, etc.) are officially supported **today**. |


#### Marketing and growth (typical expectations)

- **SEO-friendly** storefront claims; on-page merchandising within template constraints.
- **Promotions** aligned with marketplace campaigns (vouchers, flash sales) from multichannel sellers’ workflows.
- Training, workshops, and **seller education** emphasized for Malaysian SMEs.

#### “Small” differentiators vs global SaaS

- **Local courier and payment** integrations are first-class relative to generic US builders.
- **Heavy marketplace sellers** use SiteGiant for **ops** (one inventory truth) more than for **blog SEO**.

### Pricing (secondary sources — verify with sales)

Third-party reviews (2024) cited **webstore** plans roughly:

- **Mini:** ~RM 105 / month (billed yearly)
- **Basic:** ~RM 155 / month (yearly)
- **Pro:** ~RM 175 / month (yearly)

with limits such as **orders/month**, **SKU counts**, and **staff seats** on lower tiers. **Do not rely on these numbers without a SiteGiant quote**; enterprise modules are typically custom.

### Strengths

- **Marketplace-native** workflows and Malaysian seller support ecosystem.
- **Operations depth** (ERP/WMS) if you outgrow “simple” storefront tools.
- Often recommended in local communities when **Shopee sync** and ops are priorities.

### Weaknesses / watch-outs

- Some reviews note **dated templates** or **builder performance**; judge with a live trial.
- **Annual billing** emphasis may reduce flexibility vs monthly SaaS.
- Feature set is broad — **implementation and training** time can be non-trivial.

## Quick comparison (rule of thumb)


| If you prioritize…                                  | Lean toward…                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Lowest DIY stack cost                               | WooCommerce on budget hosting (with time to maintain)        |
| Fast global DTC brand                               | Shopify + apps                                               |
| MYR plans + Shopee/TikTok/Lazada + POS in one stack | EasyStore or SiteGiant (trial both)                          |
| Warehouse + multichannel ERP                        | SiteGiant ecosystem (or evaluate dedicated ERP + middleware) |
