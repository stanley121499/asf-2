# Self-hosted and niche platforms (brief appendix)

**Updated:** April 2026  

Less common for **absolute beginners** than Shopify or marketplaces, but appear in Malaysian agency and hosting bundles.

## PrestaShop / OpenCart (PHP, self-hosted)

- **Model:** Free/open-source core; you pay **hosting**, **themes**, and **modules**.
- **Strengths:** No platform transaction fee; customizable; some Malaysian hosts advertise one-click installs.
- **Watch-outs:** Security updates, module quality variance, developer skills needed for serious catalogs.

## Magento Open Source / Adobe Commerce

- **Model:** **Magento Open Source** is free software; **Adobe Commerce** is enterprise-licensed with professional services.
- **Audience:** Large catalogs, B2B, complex integrations — usually **not** the first choice for a solo hawker stall going online.
- **Watch-outs:** Hosting cost, specialist developers, TCO dominates.

## GoDaddy / Square Online / Ecwid-style embeds

- **GoDaddy:** Website builder + commerce; simple SKUs; check **Malaysian payment** support before committing.
- **Square Online:** Strong where **Square** payments and US-centric retail exist; less central in Malaysia than in the US.
- **Ecwid / embeddable carts:** Add commerce to an existing site or social; fees depend on plan tier and payment processor.

## Why this appendix matters for ASF-2

Competitive framing is not only **Shopify vs WooCommerce**; Malaysian SMEs also evaluate **local omnichannel SaaS** and sometimes **open-source + cheap hosting**. Feature expectations (FPX, DuitNow, Shopee sync, POS) come from that mix.
