# Other global SaaS competitors (Wix, Squarespace, BigCommerce)

**Updated:** April 2026  
**Audience:** Malaysian merchants evaluating **hosted** alternatives to Shopify, especially when they want a **site builder** first or **B2B / scale** features.

Currency below is mostly **USD** unless noted. Use your bank’s FX for MYR.

---

## Wix eCommerce

**Site:** [https://www.wix.com/ecommerce/website](https://www.wix.com/ecommerce/website)

### What you get

- **Drag-and-drop** site builder with **store** features (catalog, cart, payments, appointments in the broader Wix ecosystem).
- **Wix Payments** in a **limited set of countries**; otherwise connect third-party processors.
- **App Market** extends shipping, reviews, subscriptions, etc.

### Functions and integrations (granular)

| Area | Typical capabilities |
|------|----------------------|
| **Facebook / Instagram** | **Wix + Meta** integrations: connect business page, **Facebook Pixel**, **Instagram** linking, social posting from dashboard; **Facebook Shop**-style catalog features depend on Meta approval and region. Search Wix Help for “Facebook” / “Instagram” for current steps. |
| **Google** | **Google Business Profile** connection, **Search Console** / **Analytics** integrations in **Wix SEO Wiz** / marketing tools; **Google Ads** campaign creation from Wix (region-dependent). |
| **Ads pixels** | **Meta Pixel**, **Google tag**, sometimes **TikTok** via marketing integrations or custom code (Velo for advanced). |
| **Email & CRM** | **Wix Ascend** / **email marketing** (contacts, automations, newsletters)—often a **separate subscription** tier from hosting. |
| **SEO** | SEO Wiz, structured data basics, sitemap, editable meta; **blog** for content. |
| **Bookings & services** | **Wix Bookings**, **Wix Restaurants**, etc., can sit beside a small store on one site plan stack. |
| **Marketplaces** | Usually **limited** vs SiteGiant; may rely on third-party apps or manual listing. |
| **Shipping** | Built-in shipping regions + **App Market** (Shippo, etc.) for labels/rates where available. |

### Pricing pattern (USD — verify)

Public third-party summaries (2025–2026) commonly cite commerce-capable plans starting roughly **USD 27–39 / month** on annual billing (plan names have changed over time: Core / Business / Business Elite, etc.).  

**Transaction fees:** Many analyses state Wix charges **no extra platform transaction fee** on commerce plans and you pay **payment processing** fees — **confirm** on Wix’s help center for **your country** and plan, because product packaging evolves.

### Strengths

- **Visual editing** is approachable for non-developers.
- Potentially **lower fee stacking** than Shopify when not using Shopify Payments (depends on your country and processor).

### Weaknesses / watch-outs

- **Wix Payments country list** may exclude Malaysia; you’ll rely on **third-party gateways** and available apps.
- **Data portability** and deep commerce customization can be limiting vs WooCommerce.
- **Performance** depends on template and app load; test mobile Core Web Vitals.

## Squarespace Commerce

**Site:** [https://www.squarespace.com/pricing](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing)

### What you get

- **Design-forward** templates; strong fit for **brands, creatives, services** that also sell products.
- **Squarespace Payments** where available; plan-dependent **commerce transaction fees** on some tiers.
- **Digital products**, memberships, and scheduling exist across the broader Squarespace product family (sometimes as add-ons).

### Functions and integrations (granular)

| Area | Typical capabilities |
|------|----------------------|
| **Facebook / Instagram** | Connect accounts for **social links** and **shopping** where Meta approves; help docs cover **Meta Pixel** and **dynamic ads** hooks on commerce-capable plans. |
| **Google** | **Search Engine Optimization** panel (titles, descriptions); integrations for **Google Ads** / **Analytics** appear on higher plans in rolling plan generations—confirm on your account’s feature list. |
| **Email** | **Squarespace Email Campaigns** (subscriber lists, blasts)—often billed separately from the site plan. |
| **Scheduling** | **Acuity Scheduling** (owned by Squarespace) for appointments; separate subscription. |
| **Video / content** | Strong **portfolio** and **video** limits by plan; good for brand storytelling + small catalog. |
| **Shipping** | Carrier-calculated shipping, label integrations on **higher** commerce tiers (plan-gated—verify). |
| **Extensions** | **Squarespace Extensions** marketplace for select third-party tools (accounting, dropshipping, etc.)—smaller than Shopify’s app store. |

### Pricing pattern (USD — verify)

Squarespace has been **rolling out new plan names** (Basic / Core / Plus / Advanced) in some regions; legacy plans (Personal / Business / Commerce Basic / Advanced) still appear for existing customers.

**Commerce transaction fees (conceptual):**

- Some **lower** website tiers charge a **percent fee** on physical product sales (for example **~2–3%** depending on plan generation).
- Higher commerce tiers move toward **0% platform transaction fee** but still charge **payment processing** fees.

**Digital goods** can carry **separate** platform fees on some plans (help center discusses digital product fees separately).

### Strengths

- **Polished design** with less tinkering than WordPress.
- Good when the website is **half editorial, half shop**.

### Weaknesses / watch-outs

- **Malaysian payment methods** may require compatible processors; verify FPX / local wallets coverage for your checkout path.
- **Fee structure** is easy to misunderstand — model **total fees** before launch.

### Sources

- [Squarespace pricing](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing)
- [Transaction fees and payment processing rates (help center)](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/articles/27853679334157)

## BigCommerce

**Site:** [https://www.bigcommerce.com/essentials/pricing](https://www.bigcommerce.com/essentials/pricing)

### What you get

- **Hosted** commerce with **no additional platform transaction fee** on Essentials plans (per BigCommerce pricing messaging); you still pay **payment provider** fees.
- Strong **B2B** and **multi-channel** positioning in mid-market.
- **Annual online sales caps** per plan tier: crossing them **auto-upgrades** your plan (trailing 12-month GMV).

### Functions and integrations (granular)

| Area | Typical capabilities |
|------|----------------------|
| **Channel manager** | Native emphasis on **multi-channel** listing: connect **Facebook**, **Instagram**, **Google** (Shopping / Ads surfaces), **Amazon**, **eBay**, **Walmart** (US-centric), etc.—exact connectors vary by region and API maintenance. |
| **Meta** | Product/catalog sync and pixel-style advertising support via built-in channel integrations (verify **Malaysia** availability for each connector). |
| **Google** | **Google Shopping** / Ads feed patterns similar in spirit to Shopify’s Google channel; use BigCommerce’s **Google BigCommerce** integration docs for current steps. |
| **SEO** | Editable metadata, microdata, CDN, **AMP** options (theme-dependent); blogging built in. |
| **B2B** | **Price lists**, **customer groups**, **net terms**-style features on **Plus** and up (enterprise positioning). |
| **Email marketing** | Integrations with **Mailchimp**, **Klaviyo**, **Omnisend**, etc.; abandoned cart on **Plus** historically—confirm current plan gates. |
| **POS** | Partner ecosystem (e.g. **Square**, **Vend** / Lightspeed history)—not as vertically integrated as Shopify POS. |
| **WordPress headless** | **BigCommerce for WordPress** plugin exists for content-on-WordPress + checkout on BigCommerce (advanced architecture). |

### Pricing pattern (USD — verify)

Essentials pricing (annual framing commonly advertised):

| Plan | Approx. USD / month (annual) | Online sales / year cap (vendor rule) |
|------|-------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Standard | ~29 | Up to ~50k USD |
| Plus | ~79 | Up to ~180k USD |
| Pro | ~299 | Up to ~400k USD (with step-ups beyond) |
| Enterprise | Custom | High GMV |

Month-to-month billing is typically **higher** per month than annual.

### Strengths

- **Predictable** for merchants who hate Shopify-style **third-party payment surcharges**.
- **Scales** with more native commerce features than pure site builders.

### Weaknesses / watch-outs

- **Sales caps** mean growing stores face **automatic** price jumps.
- **Theme / frontend** ecosystem is smaller than Shopify’s.
- Same **local payment** homework as any global hosted player in Malaysia.

### Sources

- [BigCommerce Essentials pricing](https://www.bigcommerce.com/essentials/pricing)

## When Malaysian merchants pick these over Shopify

- **Wix / Squarespace:** Brand-first sites, smaller catalogs, strong visual control, less appetite for app stacks.
- **BigCommerce:** Catalog complexity, B2B, or annoyance with **third-party gateway surcharges** on other platforms (always still compare **gateway availability** in MY).
